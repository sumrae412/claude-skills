# Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) — Role Family Profile

Load this when a JD classifies as archetype **G. Forward Deployed / Applied AI Engineer**
in `role-archetypes.md`, or whenever the target title contains *forward deployed*,
*applied AI engineer*, *solutions engineer*, or *deployment engineer*. It carries the
empirical shape of the role plus Summer-specific positioning so her resume leads with
the builder-in-front-of-customers story, not a generic "AI leader" story.

## 1. What the market data shows

Source: [AI Engineering Field Guide — "Forward Deployed Engineers: What Our Job Data
Shows"](https://github.com/alexeygrigorev/ai-engineering-field-guide/blob/main/role/06-fde.md)
(146 unique FDE postings from 94 companies, Feb–Jul 2026). Treat these as employer
demand signals, not a day-in-the-life.

**The role is growing fast.** FDE listings grew 4.2x in six months (28 → 118) while the
broader AI-engineering market grew 2.3x. Still small (~3.6% of listings) but no longer a
rounding error. It shows up at model labs (Anthropic, Mistral, Databricks) and equally at
enterprise-software, consulting, logistics, and healthcare firms — not just frontier labs.

**What FDEs actually do** (share of postings mentioning each):

- 90% — build or deploy **production systems** (not demos)
- 88% — work **directly with customers/clients**
- 64% — **integrate** systems, APIs, or data
- 52% — **scope requirements / run discovery**
- 49% — **evaluation, testing, or monitoring**
- 31% — **feed field lessons back into the product**
- 29% — prototypes / POCs / demos
- 9% — travel or onsite

The combination is the point: discover the problem, integrate the customer's systems,
write production code, and stay on the hook for whether it works. 91% are classified
customer-facing.

**Skills employers ask for** (share of postings):

- Python 91% · Prompt engineering 55% · RAG 52% · AWS 47% · LLMs 43% · AI agents 42%
- Docker 40% · GCP 38% · Kubernetes 35% · Azure 34% · CI/CD 34% · LangChain 33%

Applied, not research-heavy. Because FDEs deploy into whatever stack the customer already
runs, all three clouds show up — they can't assume one environment.

**Seniority.** 107 of 146 titles carry no level marker; of those that do: 19 Senior,
8 Principal, 6 Staff, 5 Lead, 1 Founding, **0 Junior/entry**. Even the unmarked roles ask
for production ownership and customer judgment. This is not an entry-level title.

**Adjacent titles that are the same work** — search and screen for these too:
*Applied AI Engineer*, *Solutions Engineer*, *Deployment Engineer*, *Forward Deployed
Researcher*. The FDE count is a lower bound on this style of work.

## 2. The tension for a leadership search — and how to resolve it

FDE is **91% individual-contributor** and only **11% management**. Summer is targeting
leadership. Do not paper over this; resolve it deliberately:

- **Target the senior slice of the IC ladder as leadership-adjacent.** The Senior /
  Staff / Principal / Lead / Founding roles (39 of 146) carry technical authority,
  customer ownership, and often de-facto team leadership without a "Manager" title. For
  Summer these are legitimate leadership targets, framed as *the person who owns the
  hardest customer deployments and sets how they get done*.
- **Or target the explicit-management framings** — *Head of Forward Deployed
  Engineering*, *FDE Manager*, *Director, Applied AI*, *Forward Deployed Researcher,
  Director* (a real posting in the sample). These are rarer; when one appears it is a
  strong fit for her director-level history and should be prioritized.
- **Either way, the resume story is builder-who-owns-customer-outcomes**, not strategist
  or governance leader. FDE hiring managers screen out candidates who can talk about AI
  but can't stand up a working system inside a customer's business under ambiguity.

## 3. What to foreground for Summer on an FDE-family JD

- **Production ownership end to end** — discovery → integration → deployed system →
  eval/monitoring → still responsible when it breaks. Bullets should show her carrying a
  build from ambiguous customer problem to running system, with a measurable outcome.
- **Direct customer/stakeholder work as an engineer**, not as a PM or advisor — scoping
  with the customer, then building the thing. This is where her builder + communicator
  combination is the differentiator most keyword-matching candidates lack.
- **The applied LLM stack**: Python, RAG, agents, prompt engineering, plus deploy-side
  fluency (a cloud + Docker/K8s + CI/CD + API/data integration). Match the JD's named
  cloud; don't assume AWS.
- **Feeding field lessons back into the product** — the 31% signal is a differentiator
  and maps cleanly to her product background; use it to bridge FDE work and product sense
  without drifting into a PM identity.

**Downplay** for this family: curriculum/education framing, thought-leadership/workshop
positioning as the headline, governance-first language, and any strategy narration not
tied to a shipped system.

## 4. Title-field lead for the FDE family

Per the LinkedIn title-field mismatch finding (recruiters filter on the structured
Experience title, not the headline), the FDE-family lead is the **builder-and-customer**
title, not "Curriculum Product Manager" or a pure "AI Director" headline. For FDE /
Applied AI / Solutions Engineer targets, lead the resume and LinkedIn Experience title
with an applied-engineering framing (e.g. *Forward Deployed / Applied AI Engineer*,
*Applied AI Solutions Lead*). This is the FDE-family member of the "two resume leads by
family" split — keep it distinct from the education/DevRel lead, which optimizes for a
different filter.
